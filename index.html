// Work · Apps

Pourtrait: every great cup has a name.

A coffee discovery app that credits the barista who made your cup, not just the café. Designed and built by RadTech, live on iOS and Android.

Visit pourtrait.nl ↗ Google Play ↗ App Store ↗

// What it does

Three sides of a cup
Drinker, barista and café each have a profile, so credit lands on the person who poured.
Follow and find
Follow the baristas you trust, log the cups they pour, and find them wherever they work next.
Claim your profile
Baristas and cafés claim their pages. Ratings only show after a consent-gated claim.
Trust and safety from day one
Blocking, reporting, moderation and duplicate-profile detection shipped in the first release.

// How RadTech built it

Stack
React Native with Expo on Supabase. PostGIS drives location-aware search for nearby baristas and venues.
Data
A live specialty-coffee dataset with 7,800+ cities on the map, from Amsterdam to Berlin, London and Paris.
Languages
English, Dutch, German and French.
Status
Live on Google Play and the App Store, with an early-access list for cities not yet mapped.
