Pourtrait · coffee
Product · solo founder · 2026 · in development

Pourtrait — rate the barista, not the cafe

A discovery app for specialty coffee that scores the person who made your drink, not just the venue it came from, with live shift-aware search for who is actually working right now.

  • Location-aware discovery of nearby baristas and venues, built on a live NL specialty-coffee dataset.
  • Claim-your-profile identity and reputation: consent-gated scoring so a barista's rating only shows once they've claimed it.
  • Trust and safety built in from the start: blocking, reporting, moderation, and duplicate-profile detection.

Kawa · routing
Product · solo founder · 2026 · in development

Kawa — a community road-intelligence layer for motorcyclists

An AI route planner that finds the good roads, not just the fast ones, and quietly learns which roads are actually worth riding from the community that rides them.

  • Plans curvy, scenic, toll- and highway-aware routes on open map data, then exports turn-by-turn GPX for offline navigation. No API key, no build step.
  • Builds a living road-quality map from anonymous, blurred road geometry: privacy-first by design, with no tracking of individual trips.
  • Private and group ride sharing, a lightweight rider identity, and a merit system that rewards mapping and rating real roads.

Kawa · ride atlas
Interactive studies · 2026 · vanilla JS, zero dependencies

Kawa Ride Atlas — learn the machine, then the physics

Two interactive plates that teach a motorcycle from the inside out, built as design studies for the Kawa brand.

  • Exploded parts atlas — pull the bike apart component by component; click any part to read what it does and why it is shaped that way. 23 parts across 6 systems.
  • Lean & cornering simulator — cornering as a live physics problem: pick a body position, change speed, radius and grip, and watch the lean angle and force vectors fall out of the maths in real time.

agent-ken · orchestration
AI tooling · personal · 2026 · Python

agent-ken — a parallel, self-reviewing agent orchestrator

A dependency-aware kanban that takes a single brief, turns it into a plan, and ships it with a fleet of AI agents, without me babysitting each step.

  • Decomposes a task into subtasks with explicit dependencies, then runs the independent ones in parallel waves.
  • Gates every subtask through an automated reviewer and only marks work done once it passes: quality built into the loop.
  • Generates live kanban, mind-map and flow visualisations of the whole run as self-contained HTML dashboards.
